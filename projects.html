Transition Resource Circle

An assemblage of activists, artists, funders and cosmologists working to alchemize and liberate wealth in service to life-affirming systems – building movements that embody the transition from extractive logics toward regenerative and liberatory ways of being.

Project: Creation of TRC's core visual brand, with ongoing design support across Instagram, publications, webinars, and illustrations – plus visual narrative and brand identity for Post Capitalist Philanthropy, a book and immersive website.

SDI

SDI is a global network of community-based organisations driving bottom-up change for inclusive cities, connecting the urban poor across Africa, Asia and Latin America.

Project: Visual identity and handbook publication design – including infographics and editorial layout – for Know Your City, a global community mapping project.

Post Capitalist Philanthropy

A critique of modern philanthrocapitalism by Alnoor Ladha and Lynn Murphy, a book exploring The Healing of Wealth in the Time of Collapse.

Project: Information design, book design, website design, and branding for Post Capitalist Philanthropy – original graphics distilling complex concepts throughout the book, extended into a visual narrative framework across the website, workshops, and presentations to philanthropic organisations.

Circular Neighbourhoods

The Goethe-Institut’s Sustainable Together residency on Circular Neighbourhoods.

Project: Infographic design for Mapping Community-Led Neighbourhood Resilience in Makers Valley – translating grassroots socio-ecological initiatives into accessible tools that surface and amplify community resilience.

Grassroots Economics

Grassroots Economics is a non-profit foundation partnering with communities to take charge of their own livelihoods and economic future through local resource coordination and alternative economic systems.

Project: Information design, layout, and illustration for the Grassroots Economics: Reflection and Practice book, designed in both English and French. Illustration with Tamsin Lotz.

Culture Hack Labs & Earthrise Studio

Culture Hack Labs is a not-for-profit consultancy supporting movements, organisations and activists to shift the cultural narratives that underpin ecological breakdown, extractivism and inequality.

Earthrise Studio is a social-first, impact-driven media company creating digital content and documentaries that shift cultural narratives on the climate crisis and humanity’s relationship with the natural world.

Project: Publication and communication design for The Carbon Fixation & Climate Philanthropy reports.

CIVICUS

CIVICUS is a global alliance of civil society organisations advocating for civic space and citizen action worldwide.

Project: Infographic design for the contributions behind the UN General Comment 37 for Freedom of Peaceful Assembly.

Arkology

Arkology is a purpose-led studio designing technologies, protocols and systems for commons-oriented collaboration – building the cultural and digital infrastructure for a transition toward thriving, post capitalist ecologies.

Project: Website and visual identity design for Arkology. Designed with Simone Naude.
