Co-founder of OCTOPI

Social Design Studio dedicated to collective action against systemic oppression. We work to reimagine more inclusive futures through narrative activism, systems design and solidarity.

Design is never neutral. It governs how we see the world, what we notice and what we don't, how we move through spaces, which narratives feel true, and whose voices get left out. It shapes how we think, whether we act, and what we believe is possible.

As a designer, I hold that responsibility close, humanising data and translating complex research into visual narratives with intention and care. My work lives at the intersection of social design, information design, and storytelling, and extends into grassroots spaces, bridging design education into local, real-world contexts.

I'm committed to creating work that doesn't add to the noise but cuts through it, shifting narratives and inspiring collective social change.

Based in Cape Town & Mexico City.
